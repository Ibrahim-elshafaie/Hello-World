# Design brief: Villa 2D175B (Rockwood, type TH-M)

This brief holds the design assumptions. Edit it and ask Claude to update `design.json` to match.

## Household (assumed)

- A couple in the master bedroom
- Bedroom 1 and Bedroom 2 for children or guests, each with a desk
- Live-in help using the maid room and maid bath
- Two cars in the parking area

## Style

Warm contemporary: calm, easy to clean, nothing too trendy.

- Walls: warm off-white
- Floors: large-format light porcelain downstairs, light oak-look flooring in bedrooms
- Wood: light oak veneer for wardrobes, TV unit and dining table
- Upholstery: sand and greige performance fabrics
- Accents: matte black metal, olive green and terracotta cushions, plants

## Layout rules used

- Main walkways at least 900 mm where the room allows, 600 mm minimum in the maid room
- No furniture inside a door swing
- TV viewing distance about 2.3 to 3 m
- Dining for 6
- Existing kitchen counters and bathroom fixtures stay where the plan shows them

- Exact household size and ages
